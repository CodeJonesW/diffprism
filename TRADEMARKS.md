## What you can do without asking

**Nominative use.** You may use the name "DiffPrism" to refer truthfully to
this project, as long as you don't suggest the Project Owner sponsors or
endorses you or your product. For example:

- "Compatible with DiffPrism"
- "A fork of DiffPrism"
- "Works with DiffPrism's commit gate"
- Blog posts, tutorials, talks, reviews, and news coverage about DiffPrism

Use the plain word mark in text. Don't use the logo, and don't make "DiffPrism"
more prominent than your own name.

**Unmodified redistribution.** You may redistribute unmodified official
releases under the name DiffPrism.

## Forks and modified versions

You may fork and modify the code under the terms of its license, but a
modified version you distribute publicly must use a different name and logo.
Specifically, don't use "DiffPrism", or a confusingly similar name, as:

- the name of your fork, product, or service
- your npm package name, GitHub organization or repository name, or domain
  name
- part of a logo or icon

Describing your project as "a fork of DiffPrism" or "based on DiffPrism" is
fine, and encouraged.
